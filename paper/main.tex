\section*{Ethical considerations}
This work audits a dual-use failure mode: activation interventions can reveal and causally increase unsafe tool-action rates. Experiments use simulated tools and benchmark environments; no real user account, production tool, or external system is modified by a generated action. The design evaluates benign capability alongside unsafe-action effects.  It does not contain model weights or an executable attack service. The study recruits no human participants and performs no new human annotation; its benign anchors reuse screened inputs from a public dataset. Model-based evaluation is explicitly disclosed and audited for disagreement and missingness.

\paragraph{Use of AI tools.}
Generative AI tools were used to assist with code development, experiment
orchestration, documentation,  and editing this manuscript. Generative AI systems were also used as model-based evaluators, as described in \cref{sec:semantic-evaluation}; their identities, missingness, and disagreements are retained as measurement metadata rather than treated as ground truth. The authors take responsibility for the final content of the work, including all AI-assisted artifacts.
